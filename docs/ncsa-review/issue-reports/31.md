2. **Exceptions pin the expected cause**, as `{(2008, 10): Septicemia, (2012, 0): Total_Deaths}`, rather than only a position. This closes the 2008 Nephritis/Septicemia swap: pdfplumber merges "syndromeSepticemia" into column 9, so column 9 matched either way.

4. **Change exceeds the plan's "about 0.6%"**: psychiatry 2008 and 2009 move +0.74%, and 2020 moves -0.62%. A small rate near 11 makes the 2-decimal figure move further. Logged, not stopped.
